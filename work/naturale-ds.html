The design system behind this website and my resumé. Five colours from nature, one type pairing and a few rules that keep everything feeling like it grew in the same place.

Spacing

Room to breathe

Nine steps on a 4px grid. The same steps space text, cards and sections.

  • 4Icon to label, tag dots
  • 8Inside chips and buttons, label to field
  • 12Between list items and form fields
  • 16Card padding on phones, between related cards
  • 24Card padding on desktop, heading to content
  • 32Between groups inside a section
  • 48Between sub-sections, like the palette and semantic colours
  • 64Between sections on phones
  • 96Between sections on desktop

A 4px base so spacing lines up with the type grid. Within a group, closer means related; each step out adds air between ideas.

Iconography

Drawn with one pen

  • One grid and stroke. 24px grid, 2px stroke, round caps and joins.
  • Colour follows text. Icons inherit their colour and turn Moss when active.
  • Icons for actions, not labels. Go, download, copy and contact get icons. Text that already reads clearly does not.
  • A little play. Category icons wiggle with a spring when you touch them.

Motion

Things move like air

Hover or tap a card to play it.

  • Gust

    cubic-bezier(.34, 1.56, .64, 1)

    Quick, with a little overshoot. For things you touch: toggles, tabs, arrows.

  • Breeze

    cubic-bezier(.2, .8, .2, 1)

    Fast in, slow to settle. For things that arrive: sheets, cards, text.

  • Wind

    opacity + rise, .26s

    Fades out and back in. For text that changes in place.

  • Still

    prefers-reduced-motion

    No movement. Every animation has a still version.

Built with Claude

How I used AI to take it further

I used Claude, Anthropic’s AI model, as a design partner for version two. I set the direction and made every call. Claude explored options quickly, explained the trade-offs and turned decisions into working code.

  • Amrit

    Setting the brief

    A palette inspired by nature: greens to lead, browns for text, beige for backgrounds. Type and every later decision had to fit that idea.

  • ClaudeAmrit

    Choosing the type

    Claude set eight pairings in my own name and palette so I could compare them in context. I chose Young Serif with Instrument Sans.

  • ClaudeAmrit

    A scale with reasons

    Claude derived modular scales for web and print and explained each ratio. I adjusted the resumé sizes for how recruiters actually read: on screen.

  • AmritClaude

    Purging the palette

    I cut the colours down to five and kept my original Moss. Claude checked every pairing against WCAG and flagged where Moss can’t carry small text.

  • Claude

    Accessibility audits

    Claude measured contrast over the moving gradient, found failures I had sensed but not proven, and fixed them in both themes.

  • AmritClaude

    Building and refining

    Claude coded the site, water and motion. I art-directed every round, from navbar spacing to alignment to how the letters surface.

AI made exploring fast. The taste and the final call stayed mine.
